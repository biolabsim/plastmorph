# Simulation Model

The mass model uses first-order decay:

$$
M(t) = M_0 e^{-kt}
$$

Where:

- $M_0$ is initial mass
- $k$ is the effective degradation rate
- $t$ is time in years

The effective rate combines:

- material-specific baseline degradation resistance
- environment pressure from UV, moisture, oxygen, microbes, abrasion, and temperature
- material-environment interaction modifiers
